# references

What the system is built on, pulled from Bassim's own X bookmarks and Raindrop collections (me.db, Oct 8 2026), plus a few outside references that clearly fit. One line each on what to take.

## From his saved posts and bookmarks

1. **Neo Industrial: "the perfect collision of Swiss design and technical spec sheets"**, @kyleanthony. https://x.com/kyleanthony/status/2093309881893724669. The thesis in one line: Swiss grid and restraint, spec-sheet tables and labels. The ledger and the rubric come from here.
2. **markdown graphs: "tables, charts, and diagrams for MDX that look like they were typed"**, @kshvbgde. https://x.com/kshvbgde/status/2093015885669888237. The diagram and chart style: typed, mono, 1px, no icons.
3. **Swiss / International Style + editorial brutalism, "a rational skeleton holding strong emotion"**, @xiaoxiaodong01. https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2092617208488948195. Why one red on paper is enough emotion for a strict grid.
4. **A TUI design tool: "design directly on the terminal grid, ASCII borders that join automatically"**, @arvsrn (opencode). https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2092181600658485489. The box-drawing tree as a first-class visual, not a code sample.
5. **terminal.shop**, Raindrop › webDev Inspiration. https://www.terminal.shop/. A whole brand carried by a mono face, a prompt and almost nothing else; proof that "terminal" can be warm and sellable.
6. **Vintage annual report covers**, @TJ_Pratt and @oliverhamrin ("peaked in 1977"). https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2088105648521134168 · https://x.com/oliverhamrin/status/2088376503914426653. Numbers as the hero, tabular figures, dates as stamps; the ledger's tone.
7. **US Graphics TX-02 / Berkeley Mono printed specimens**, @fatih. https://x.com/fatih/status/2061033715891917088. The specimen page as a brand artefact, and the mono he actually admires; Berkeley is the paid upgrade path for the mono.
8. **"Just width + line height can completely change your design"**, @AdamBartas. https://x.com/AdamBartas/status/2093382621623681449. Measure 64ch and line-height 1.55 are doing the work; nothing else needed.
9. **"i love 14px a lot"**, @nachidesigner. https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2086865987803185255. The working size for every mono surface.
10. **An 8px base grid, "set up as tightly as possible"**, @MSchwaibold. https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2096306405649318139. The spacing scale.
11. **"0.5 stroke is brutally underrated"**, @_heyrico. https://x.com/_heyrico/status/1972262632611545242. Hairlines at low alpha instead of grey borders; one solid 1px only where it matters.
12. **`text-box-trim` is Baseline**, @ipwanciu. https://x.com/ipwanciu/status/2092977432957477319. Headlines sit flush to rules in the real site; the specimen does not use it yet.
13. **"A minimalist indicator at the top can be much cleaner than a big tooltip"**, @wherescz. https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2098933026101096640. Charts show the one value in red; no hover cards.
14. **Making Software**, Raindrop › webDev Inspiration. https://www.makingsoftware.com/. Paper, mono, hand-set diagrams that explain mechanisms; the closest living site to this system's feel.
15. **Rauno's Web Interface Guidelines**, Raindrop. https://interfaces.rauno.me/. The motion and interaction rules (durations, reduced motion, no scroll hijack) are his list applied.
16. **Mitchell Hashimoto's site** and **Anthony Fu's site**, Raindrop. https://mitchellh.com/ · https://antfu.me/. Developer personal sites that are text first and still have a strong identity; the register the commonplace site should sit in.
17. **"obsessed with the archive vibe on this website"**, @Aurelien_Gz on surd.studio. https://x.com/Aurelien_Gz/status/2068348577496097077. The archive as an aesthetic: dated entries, indexes, lists.
18. **fieldtheory: a CLI that syncs your X bookmarks locally "so your agent can access them"**, @andrewfarah. https://x.com/andrewfarah/status/2040535589771149379. A cousin product he saved; what the category already looks like, and what commonplace should not look like (one source, one tool).
19. **ASCII backgrounds are trending**, @kail_designs, @uihssn; and the counterpoint, @hewarsaber: "unlike the bs ascii art flooding the timeline". https://x.com/kail_designs/status/2063524629571064128 · https://x.com/hewarsaber/status/2044350699979333727. He saved both: the terminal look is in, and ASCII wallpaper is already a cliché. So: box-drawing where it carries meaning (the tree), never as texture.
20. **Linear's new icons and components**, @Griveau. https://twitter.com/i/web/status/2090746237934883043. If icons ever appear: this weight and restraint.

## Outside references that fit

- **IBM Plex** specimen and the reasoning behind the family (https://www.ibm.com/plex/). Mono and sans drawn together; why one family carries both voices.
- **Locke's "A New Method of Making Common-Place-Books" (1706)**. The original index-and-ledger method; the reason the brand is tables and dates, not notebooks and quills.
- **Light terminal themes** (GitHub Light, Rosé Pine Dawn). The palette's role model: one warm paper, muted ANSI colours that only appear in output.

## What was deliberately not taken

- The hex site's neo-brutalist kit: thick 2px borders, hard offset shadows, yellow/pink/blue blocks, stickers. Right for hex, wrong for a template you read and fork.
- Glassmorphism, gradients, 3D hero objects, isometric icons, dark-mode-first "hacker" terminals, ASCII art as background.
